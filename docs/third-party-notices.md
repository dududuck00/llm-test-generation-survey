# Third-party attribution

The ACM template files in `manuscript/` (`acmart.cls`, `acmart.dtx`, `acmart.ins`, and `ACM-Reference-Format.bst`) were supplied in the user's `acmart-primary.zip` and are retained unchanged. Their original copyright notices and `manuscript/LICENSE` apply.

Bibliographic entries and linked publications are attributed through titles, authors, citation IDs, DOI/arXiv records, and source URLs. Third-party paper PDFs are not redistributed in this repository.

Figure glyphs are native geometric reconstructions, not a downloaded icon collection. Fonts are resolved from the build environment and are not bundled. The preferred fonts are Times New Roman and Consolas; compatible system-font alternatives can be used on other platforms.

No additional repository-wide license has been assigned to the newly authored research notes, manuscript, or scripts. Adding a reuse license is a separate publication decision.
